What HEPA Filters Actually Do

A HEPA filter stands for High-Efficiency Particulate Air and can capture particles as small as 0. 3 microns. For context, bacteria are typically 0. 5 to 5 microns and viruses are 0. 05 to 0. 2 microns (though viruses typically travel on larger carrier particles). When air passes through a HEPA-equipped scrubber, the filter traps these microscopic threats with remarkable efficiency: true HEPA filters catch 99. 97 percent of airborne contaminants.

Edwardsville, IL residents often assume that replacing their home HVAC filter is sufficient. Standard furnace filters are designed for dust and pollen, not biohazard particles. They block large debris but let most biological contaminants pass straight through. Professional HEPA filtration is several orders of magnitude more effective.

HVAC systems in Edwardsville homes distribute air throughout the building, which means contaminated air spreads efficiently through ductwork and return air paths. Professionals address HVAC contamination by sealing or isolating the system during cleanup, then thoroughly disinfecting ductwork and components. Air scrubbers positioned near return vents continuously filter air before it re-enters the HVAC system.

After their Edwardsville cleanup is complete, families may need HVAC duct cleaning or disinfection to ensure pathogens are not trapped in the system. Professionals assess each property's HVAC condition and recommend appropriate remediation. Some situations require UV disinfection inside ductwork or replacement of filter media. Professional teams' goal is ensuring the air that flows through an Edwardsville home is safe and clean for months after cleanup is finished.
