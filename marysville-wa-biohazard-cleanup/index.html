Why Standard Cleaning Fails

Household cleaners and disinfectants sold in grocery stores are designed for routine household dirt, not biohazard contamination. The concentration of active ingredients in retail products is far too low to eliminate pathogenic organisms in the quantities present at a contamination site. Standard cleaning also fails to address contamination in porous materials, within building structures, and in areas that are difficult to access. Professional remediation recognizes these limitations and employs methods specifically engineered to overcome them.
