Engineering Controls and Containment Barriers

OSHA emphasizes engineering controls as the most effective approach to preventing occupational exposure. Engineering controls are physical structures or equipment that eliminate or reduce the hazard itself, rather than relying on workers to use protective equipment. For biohazard cleanup in Woodinville, engineering controls include containment barriers that prevent contaminated materials from spreading, negative air pressure systems that maintain air flow from clean areas toward contaminated areas, and HEPA filtration that removes any airborne pathogens from the air.

Professional crews in Woodinville designs containment strategies specific to each property's layout and contamination extent. Small, localized contamination may require simple plastic sheeting and duct tape containment. Larger contamination areas or situations with potential for aerosol generation may require negative air pressure equipment and HEPA filtration. These engineering controls protect both workers and the surrounding property from contamination spread during remediation work.
