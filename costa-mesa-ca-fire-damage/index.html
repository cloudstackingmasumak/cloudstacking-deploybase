Professional Cleaning for Textiles and Soft Furnishings

Clothing, bedding, curtains, and upholstered furniture saturated with soot and smoke require professional cleaning using specialized techniques. Standard laundry cannot remove embedded soot because the particles are bonded at the fiber level. Professional cleaners use extraction methods that flush soot particles out of fibers, heat treatments that drive moisture out of dense materials, and specialized detergents that break down soot bonds without damaging fabrics. Delicate items like silk, wool, or vintage textiles receive gentle dry-cleaning methods rather than harsh wet extraction. After cleaning, items are dried in climate-controlled chambers to prevent mildew and odor reabsorption. For Costa Mesa homes, the entire textile cleaning process for contents may take two to four weeks depending on the volume of items and the cleaning method required.
