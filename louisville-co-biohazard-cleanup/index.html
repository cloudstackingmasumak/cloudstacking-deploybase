Recognizing Blood and Bodily Fluid Contamination

Blood and bodily fluids are some of the most dangerous biohazards in any home or business setting and require immediate professional attention for your safety. Even small amounts of blood can contain dangerous bloodborne pathogens like HIV, hepatitis B, and hepatitis C that survive outside the body for extended periods. In Louisville, if an incident involves any visible blood or bodily fluids whatsoever, professional cleanup is absolutely necessary and non-negotiable under any circumstances. Do not attempt to clean these materials with household cleaners or standard disinfectants that are insufficient for the task. The pathogens in blood require specialized treatment and disposal procedures that standard products cannot accomplish. You cannot tell by looking at blood whether it contains dangerous pathogens or how extensive the exposure risk truly is. Professional teams have the equipment and knowledge to safely handle bloodborne materials and render them completely harmless. We use hospital-grade disinfectants and specialized equipment to eliminate pathogens completely and verify elimination through testing when necessary. Bloodborne pathogen exposure can occur through microscopic cuts you do not even realize you have.
