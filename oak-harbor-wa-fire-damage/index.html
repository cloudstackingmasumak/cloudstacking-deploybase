Insurance Coverage for Contents Restoration

Most homeowners insurance policies cover contents restoration as part of personal property coverage. Your adjuster will have to approve the scope of restoration work and the estimated costs. Keep documentation of every item you are having restored, including photographs showing the damage and the restoration process. Provide your adjuster with estimates from your restoration company. Coverage limits may apply, especially for categories like jewelry, electronics, or artwork.

Oak Harbor homeowners should ask their adjuster about replacement cost coverage for contents. Some policies offer replacement cost, which reimburses you for the full cost to replace items. Other policies offer actual cash value, which deducts depreciation. On fire claims, replacement cost coverage is far more favorable and may make professional restoration worth pursuing financially. Ask your adjuster to clarify your contents coverage before committing to restoration, especially for high-value items.

Cleaning and Deodorization Methods

Dry cleaning removes soot and smoke odor from clothing and textiles without water. Specialized solvents and equipment clean delicate fabrics like silk, wool, and antique textiles. Most clothing is dry cleaned professionally after fire.

Wet cleaning works for sturdy fabrics, linens, and items that can tolerate water. Gentler detergents than household cleaners are used. This method also removes soot and reduces smoke odor. For additional local guidance, see Helpful local resource.

Ozone treatment is sometimes applied to items that have absorbed smoke odor. Ozone penetrates fabric fibers and neutralizes odor molecules at a chemical level. This works better than surface deodorizing and reaches deep into materials.

Thermal fogging treats items in sealed chambers. Fog penetrates packaging and materials, neutralizing odors. This is effective for books, papers, and items that can't be submerged.

Ultrasonic cleaning is used on hard items like dishes, glassware, electronics, and metal objects. Ultrasonic waves loosen particles and contaminants without water or harsh chemicals.

Each item type gets the method best suited to it. Professionals determine what works for leather, rubber, plastic, metal, glass, wood, and textiles based on damage type and material properties.
