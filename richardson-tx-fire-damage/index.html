The Mold Growth Process

Mold spores are present everywhere in your Richardson home, floating in the air and settling on surfaces. When moisture arrives in the form of firefighting water, these dormant spores are activated. The spores germinate and begin growing into visible mold colonies. Different types of mold grow at different rates, but most common household molds begin visible growth within forty-eight to seventy-two hours of moisture exposure.

The most dangerous molds that grow after water damage in Richardson homes include black mold, Stachybotrys chartarum, which produces toxic substances called mycotoxins. These toxins are harmful to humans and can cause serious health problems, especially with prolonged exposure. Not all mold produces mycotoxins, but the risk of toxic mold growth is significant enough that mold prevention is critical.
