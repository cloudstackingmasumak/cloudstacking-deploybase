First-Hour Mitigation Steps

When we arrive at your Carrollton home, we immediately assess structural integrity. Is it safe to enter? Do we need to secure the roof or board up windows? Once we confirm safety, we move quickly through the house documenting damage with photographs and notes.

Simultaneously, we begin mitigation work. Tarps go over roof damage to stop rain from entering. Unsecured doors and windows are boarded to prevent further intrusion. Wet carpeting is pulled and removed if flooding occurred from firefighting efforts. Drying equipment is positioned to prevent mold. We're not waiting for an insurance adjuster to approve these steps. These are emergency measures that your homeowner policy covers.

Air handling begins immediately. We start removing water pooled on floors and in low spots where it will lead to mold if left standing. Dehumidifiers are deployed to pull moisture from the air. Drying fans create airflow that speeds evaporation. These steps are critical in the first 24 hours because mold develops fast in wet environments. Texas humidity makes this even more urgent in Carrollton homes.

Electricity to affected areas is assessed for safety. Any fire damage to wiring is dangerous, and we work with caution to avoid energizing damaged circuits. Standing water is removed to basement floor level where safe. Furniture is moved away from walls to allow drying. We're essentially putting your house into recovery mode while you make phone calls and try to process what just happened.
