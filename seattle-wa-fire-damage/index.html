Why Hardwood Floors Are Vulnerable

The aftermath of a fire brings additional threats to hardwood. Water used by firefighting crews soaks into the wood, creating swelling and warping that may develop days after the fire ends. In Seattle, homes with older hardwood flooring are particularly vulnerable because the wood has often dried significantly over the years, making it more susceptible to warping when exposed to moisture.
