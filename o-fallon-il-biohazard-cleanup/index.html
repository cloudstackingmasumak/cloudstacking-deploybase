Proper Containment and Separation Procedures for Hazmat

Before disposal, hazmat materials in O Fallon biohazard situations must be properly contained to prevent leakage, aerosolization, or exposure. Different types of hazmat require different containment approaches. Sharps must be placed in rigid, puncture-resistant containers that cannot be accidentally opened or penetrated. Contaminated soft materials like bedding, towels, or clothing must be placed in thick, durable biohazard bags that clearly indicate the contents and hazard level. Liquid waste including blood and bodily fluids requires rigid containers with tight-sealing lids to prevent spills during movement or transport. Mixed waste including contaminated items that are part solid and part liquid requires careful segregation and appropriate containers for each material type. Hazmat materials must be clearly separated from regular household waste and regular recycling to prevent accidental exposure of waste workers.

Containment is equally critical. As materials are removed from your home or business in O Fallon, they're placed in sealed bags or containers that prevent leakage or release of biological material.
